## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the quiet authority, institutional stature, and meticulous precision of an elite Indian Chartered Accountancy practice rooted in New Delhi. It deliberately rejects the hyperactive tropes of Silicon Valley fintech and generic corporate SaaS. Instead, it mirrors the physical atmosphere of a distinguished advisory chambers: aged calfskin volumes, unhurried deliberations, bespoke brass fittings, and pristine vellum ledgers.

The audience encompasses enterprise directors, high-net-worth family offices, multi-jurisdictional corporations, and regulatory bodies seeking unflinching compliance, statutory audit excellence, and strategic tax counsel. The UI must project permanence, rigorous governance adhering strictly to the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) code of ethics, and sophisticated fiscal judgment.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Classicism** with **Modern Institutional Restraint**. Crisp architectural margins, deliberate asymmetry balanced by traditional book typography, fine hair-line rules, and subdued physical materiality create an interface that feels bespoke, authoritative, and perpetually composed.

## Colors

The palette draws its foundation from classical institutional heraldry: formal navy, archival ivory, and antique gold. 

- **Primary (`#102A43`) & Deep Ground (`#0B1F33`)**: The core anchor of the visual architecture. Used for authoritative structural blocks—including master headers, footers, primary typography anchors, and decisive interactive states. Evokes institutional longevity and deep statutory weight.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C6A568`) & Warm Amber (`#D97706`)**: Applied with strict parsimony. Never for large surface floods, but reserved for decorative rules, key audit markers, subtle badge outlines, active typographic focus, and dignified micro-accents.
- **Canvas & Surfaces**: 
  - Archival Warm Ivory (`#F8F6F1`): Primary background canvas reflecting untreated rag paper.
  - Pure White (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated cards, document containers, and data sheets.
  - Light Slate Wash (`#F1F5F9`): Secondary section alternation for legislative updates and data tables.
- **Typography & Neutrals**:
  - Ink Dark (`#1E293B`): Primary editorial headings, metric values, and primary body anchors.
  - Slate Body (`#526273`): Subtext, legal disclaimers, and running body prose ensuring optimal legibility.
  - Slate Muted (`#94A3B8`): Captions, inactive controls, and secondary metadata.
- **System & Trust**: Subtle Emerald (`#18794E`) reserved strictly for statutory certifications, filing validations, and audit clearance status.

## Typography

The typographical pairing balances traditional intellectual authority with contemporary analytical utility:

1. **Serif Display & Headings (`EB Garamond`)**: Used across all major section demarcations, editorial statements, practice area titles, and firm maxims. It references legal gazettes and high-caliber financial literature. Display headings must be set with generous line heights and delicate kerning.
2. **Utilitarian Sans Body (`Inter`)**: Deployed for technical clarity across statutory disclosures, financial schedules, service lists, advisory memos, and interactive controls.
3. **Eyebrows & Case Identifiers (`eyebrow-caps`)**: Always rendered in disciplined uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.14em`) to establish clear categorical hierarchy prior to editorial serif headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an unhurried, editorial 12-column grid system that prioritizes clarity, white space, and balanced structural division.

- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max Width 1280px)**: 
  - Margins set generously to `margin: 4rem` (expanding to `6rem` for long-form case briefings).
  - Gutters calibrated at `gutter: 1.5rem` to prevent visual collision between complex statutory tables and advisory summaries.
  - Section blocks utilize substantial vertical spacing (`4rem` to `6rem`) to grant each practice division standalone significance.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px – 1024px)**:
  - Margins contract to `2rem`, gutters adjust to `1.25rem`. Two-column symmetrical service grids collapse into structured, sequential horizontal dossiers.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, <768px)**:
  - Outer margins set strictly to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), columns reflow strictly vertically into a single narrative column.
  - Typography scales down proportionally without truncating legal or statutory copy.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on intense dropshadows or floating skeuomorphic layers. Instead, depth is articulated through **tonal paper layering** and **fine architectural hair-lines**:

- **Subtle Ambient Lift**: Cards, dossiers, and compliance forms rest upon a 1px boundary (`#E2E8F0` or `#E5E7EB`) coupled with a nearly imperceptible, warm-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.04), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.03)`.
- **Structural Inset / Deep Canvas**: Heavy sections (Partner Profiles, Statutory Advisory, Regulatory Announcements) drop entirely into the deep `#102A43` or `#0B1F33` plane, reversing typography to Ivory (`#F8F6F1`) and Muted Gold (`#C6A568`) without any drop shadow.
- **Divider Rules**: 1px horizontal and vertical hair-lines in `#E2E8F0` structure multi-column disclosures, echoing the precise ruling of traditional ledger books.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a conservative **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`0.25rem` default base radius; `0.5rem` maximum for elevated surfaces). 

Ultra-rounded or pill-shaped geometries are strictly forbidden, as they introduce a casual, consumer-app tone inappropriate for statutory audit and enterprise tax governance. Form inputs, primary buttons, document downloads, and advisory cards must maintain crisp, structured corners with only minimal edge softening to ensure precise, professional framing.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action**: Solid Deep Navy (`#102A43`) background, crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` radius, 12px vertical by 24px horizontal padding. On hover, background shifts to Dark Navy (`#0B1F33`) with a subtle 1px bottom accent border in Muted Gold (`#C6A568`).
- **Secondary / Consultation**: Ghost style with a 1px solid Deep Navy or Muted Gold border (`#C6A568`), transparent background, and Navy typography. On hover, fills with Warm Ivory (`#F8F6F1`) or Light Slate Wash (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Editorial Text Links**: Inline links feature an explicit, hair-thin underline in Muted Gold spaced 3px below the baseline, transitioning to full gold typography on focus.

### Cards & Service Dossiers
- Constructed on Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border (`#E5E7EB`) and `0.375rem` radius. 
- Internal padding is disciplined (`space-xl` or `2rem`).
- Header segment includes an `eyebrow-caps` badge, followed by an `EB Garamond` title and clear, structured bulleted advisory capabilities.

### Badges & Eyebrows
- Badges use subtle, low-saturation backgrounds: Ivory (`#F8F6F1`) with a `#C6A568` border, or soft slate (`#F1F5F9`).
- Text is tracked wide (`0.14em`), rendered in uppercase `11px` Inter semi-bold.

### Forms & Declarations
- Explicit, persistent labels in `Slate Dark` (`#1E293B`) positioned strictly above the inputs.
- Input fields set against Crisp White backgrounds with a 1px `#CBD5E1` border and `0.25rem` radius.
- Focus state reveals a dual-layer highlight: a 1px solid `#102A43` border framed by a 3px soft tint ring (`rgba(198, 165, 104, 0.2)`).

### Lists & Audit Schedules
- Unordered lists avoid bright colored dots; they utilize understated 4px square bullets in Muted Gold or thin em-dashes (`—`).
- Financial fee schedules and statutory audit tables implement alternating fills using `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F6F1`, separated by 1px horizontal borders in `#E2E8F0`.

### Regulatory & Statutory Banners
- Formal disclaimer blocks (ICAI regulatory compliance notes, statutory disclosures) are enclosed in a muted ivory box with a 3px solid vertical left spine in Muted Gold (`#C6A568`), utilizing `body-sm` typography.
