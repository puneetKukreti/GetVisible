Frequently Asked Questions

Essential clarifications on engagements, consultations, and statutory advisory protocols.

What services does your CA firm provide? expand_more
We assist with the statutory services listed across this website, including direct taxation, corporate GST audits, MCA secretarial filings, Virtual CFO accounting, and NRI repatriation. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements and confirm whether the relevant service is available.
Can I consult you online? expand_more
Yes. We facilitate structured remote video consultations via Microsoft Teams and Google Meet, coupled with secure encrypted portals for digital document review, serving clients across Bangalore, Mumbai, Chennai, and overseas NRIs.
What documents are needed for income tax filing? expand_more
Requirements depend on your income sources and circumstances. Typical baseline schedules include Form 16/16A, Annual Information Statement (AIS), Form 26AS, capital gains statements from broking accounts, and interest certificates. We will share a tailored checklist upon case review.
Do you assist startups and small businesses? expand_more
Yes. We advise emerging enterprises on incorporation (Pvt Ltd, LLP), DPIIT startup tax exemptions, founder share agreements, MSME registration, and scalable monthly bookkeeping.
How are professional fees determined? expand_more
Fees depend strictly on the statutory scope, transaction complexity, turnover volume, and required partner hours. A formal quotation is agreed in writing prior to commencing work, ensuring absolute billing clarity.
How can I get started? expand_more
Submit an enquiry via our website form, call our New Delhi chambers at +91 (011) 2345-6789, or visit during office hours. Our partners will evaluate your needs and outline the next steps.
